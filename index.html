How it works

Three steps from gesture to voice

1

Show your sign

Hold your gesture in front of the webcam. MediaPipe tracks 21 hand landmarks in real time.

2

Model predicts

A 3-layer LSTM reads the motion sequence across 30 frames and identifies the sign with confidence scoring.

3

Voice speaks

Confirmed words build a sentence. Pause and the app reads it aloud. Your hands become your voice.
