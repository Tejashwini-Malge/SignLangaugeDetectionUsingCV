The technology
From gesture to voice
in under a second
1

Camera captures your hands

MediaPipe tracks 21 precise hand landmarks per frame — wrist, knuckles, fingertips — in real time.

2

LSTM model reads the motion

A deep neural network analyzes 30-frame sequences of landmark movement to recognize each sign with confidence scoring.

3

AI forms a natural sentence

Detected signs are passed to an LLM that converts ASL grammar into fluent English — adding pronouns, articles, and structure.

4

Your words are spoken aloud

Google TTS converts the sentence to audio and plays it — so everyone in the room hears you clearly.
